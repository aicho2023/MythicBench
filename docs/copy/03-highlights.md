# Highlights page `/highlights`

Source: `web/src/pages/Highlights.tsx` (page text) and
`web/src/content/highlights.json` (the cards). The first three cards also
appear on the home page.

Each card has a **title** and a **summary**, which are ours to rewrite, and
**quotes**, which are the agents' own words from the replays. Quotes stay
exactly as the model wrote them (`scripts/check_highlights.py` checks every
one against the replays), so they aren't listed here. The grey line under
each heading (model, group, pull) is fixed. Any numbers in your summary get
checked against the replay when the card lands.

If 23 cards is too many to rewrite, mark the ones to cut with `CUT`; fewer,
stronger cards is a fine outcome.
